# Application-stage receipt tracking

Enable `HR_APPLICATION_INTAKE=true` alongside the existing `HR_FOLLOWUP_AUTO_SELECT=true` and `HR_FOLLOWUP_EXTENDED_FORMS=true`. The existing five-minute worker runs this flow. It covers applications created from 25 October 2026 13:00 UTC, including Stanley's application; it does not restart historical campaigns.

The application form 260903309464356 already has an immediate autoresponder with the starter links. This flow records that configured request, never claims inbox delivery, and does not resend the initial pack. An exact application/record identity match is required before moving Pending Review records from the application group into To be processed by Recruitment. Acceptance remains pending, with no offer, suitability decision, clearance, training booking or employment authorisation made automatically.

The existing HR requested/received controls display starter/DBS details (5), history (1), working-time choice (4), and, for declared licence holders, licence information (14) and driving declaration (11). A starter form receipt does not prove DBS details are sufficient or that DBS clearance has been granted. Medical requests remain staff-gated; vaccination disclosure is not chased. Referee contact remains on the separate explicit-permission workflow.

The flow reads full archives before inferring an absence, then refreshes incrementally. Receipt identity requires a matching name plus email or date of birth. Only salted identity hashes and submission identifiers are stored in its archive. Canonical evidence is re-read before recording a receipt. Partial identities, failed/incomplete source reads, duplicate staff identities, changed contact details, invalid or empty selections and uncertain deliveries block reminders. A matched history is checked using the existing completeness analyser; precise missing periods/details appear in the progress note and reminder. Other form receipts require human completeness and approval checks.

The first reminder is due 72 hours after the application creation time; at most three reminders are sent 72 hours apart from jobs@. Only outstanding applicable forms are listed. A durable reservation precedes every send; interrupted or uncertain sends are held, never retried automatically. Completion stops chasing and retains the record in Recruitment processing for human review. After enrollment, staff-selected requested and received lists are preserved. This tracker chases only the intersection of selected items with eligible application-stage forms. Extra selections (including medical, right to work and payroll) remain for their separate HR process and are never added to application emails. Progress totals describe that application-stage subset, not all selected HR requirements. Empty, unknown or duplicate selections stop chasing.

Existing holds need staff investigation. After verifying recipient, stage, selected requests and delivery history, explicitly tick HR follow-up resume. Intake consumes that tick before any release and rechecks identity, pause, acceptance/stage, source ambiguity, journal uncertainty and the three-reminder limit. Only a paused campaign can resume; delivery-uncertain, identity-review and completed campaigns are not reopened by this control. Reminder counts, due dates and receipts are preserved. Unticking pause or restarting the service alone does not resume a held campaign.

Progress is in HR follow-up update and the worker's applicationIntake state. The worker logs safe cycle counts and failure phase, without applicant answers or addresses. Disabling the feature stops intake processing; it does not delete its journal or undo emails/board changes.

## Pulse audit trail

Enable `HR_PULSE_AUDIT=true` to post dated updates to each applicant's pulse. Current-position baselines explicitly do not reconstruct earlier events. Subsequent changes record stage, acceptance, owner/due date, requested/received controls and check-status labels; intake and HR follow-up events also record receipts, reminders, holds and completion. Reminder acceptance is distinguished from inbox delivery, and receipt from approval. Raw form answers, bank details, medical answers and identity documents are excluded.

An SQLite outbox persists event keys and comparison checkpoints. Unchanged scans and restarts do not create duplicate updates. Reverting a status creates a fresh event. Milestones and live workflow changes take priority over the initial baseline backlog; a maximum of 25 posts is processed per five-minute cycle. The `pulseAudit` state exposes posted, pending and uncertain counts. An uncertain post is reconciled against its audit reference on Monday; it is never blindly resent if no matching post can be found. Existing pulse posts are appended to, never overwritten or backdated.
