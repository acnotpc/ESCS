Attendance point controls

Back to trainer portal

Only the attendance point open here can be recorded by delegates. Open lunch sign-out when lunch starts, afternoon sign-in when delegates return, and end-of-day sign-out when training finishes. Opening another point closes the previous one.

Delegates must still record their points in order. If someone missed an earlier point, review it with them. You can reopen it with a reason; their entry will retain the actual time recorded. Every change here records the trainer, time and reason. Morning sign-in is the default each booked day.
