About MMVA

Practical skills.
A person-centred purpose.

We help teams understand, prevent and respond to conflict with dignity.

Training grounded in your service.

MMVA specialises in conflict management and physical intervention. Our approach brings together positive behaviour support, trauma-informed practice and restraint reduction.

Training is led by the needs of staff and the people they support. Andrew Challoner is MMVA’s co-owner and lead trainer; Andrew and Zoe Chivers trade as MMVA.

Our starting point

A training needs analysis informs the programme, its proportionality and any adjustments for elevated risks.

Our priorities

  • Prevention and de-escalation
  • Positive relationships and communication
  • Reducing restrictive interventions
  • Reflection and learning after conflict
